\# AI Courier Tracking Assistant



An AI-powered courier tracking assistant that helps customers track shipments, estimate delivery times, and get customer support through natural language queries.



\## Project Objective



To develop an AI chatbot that can understand customer courier-tracking queries and provide shipment information and support responses.



\## Input



\- Tracking ID

\- Customer query



\## AI Concepts



\- Generative AI

\- Natural Language Processing (NLP)

\- Chatbot Development

\- Machine Learning

\- Data Analytics



\## Outputs



\- Shipment status

\- Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)

\- Customer support responses



\## Deliverables



\- AI Chatbot

\- Tracking Dashboard

\- Customer Support Portal

\- Optimization and analysis reports
